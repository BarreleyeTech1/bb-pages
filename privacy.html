Privacy policy

Barreleye Books is used only by Barreleye Technologies LLC, for its own QuickBooks Online company.

It reads that company's own bookkeeping data and, with the owner's approval, records new entries in it. That data is kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's encrypted backups. It is not sold, shared with, or sent to anyone else.

No data is collected about any other person or company. These pages use no cookies, scripts or visitor tracking.
